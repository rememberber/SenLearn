Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: High-fidelity iPadOS app UI concept for SenLearn, direction A "森间书桌".
Primary request: Design ONE complete clean flat landscape tablet screenshot in a 4:3 aspect ratio. This is the reading-and-tap-to-pronounce page of a warm, refined, offline English-learning app. Show the actual app screen edge to edge; no device frame, no perspective, no desktop backdrop, no poster, no collage, no annotations outside the UI.
Product context: Users import PDF or photos, text recognition overlays provide pronunciation on tap. Core joy comes from original book illustrations and playful response to selected words. An uncluttered native iPad experience.
Composition: a slim top toolbar, a narrow left page-thumbnail rail, and an expansive central book page occupying over 60% of the total screen. Top left: a tiny tasteful sprout icon and the word SenLearn; then back chevron and Chinese label 返回书架. Top center: current document title Forest Friends and page count 3 / 12. Right of toolbar: a restrained segmented mode control with 点读 selected and 标注 unselected, and a minimal ellipsis icon.
Main canvas: a large warm white page with subtle paper texture and soft edge shadow, nearly full remaining height and width. On the upper part of the book page, an original small editorial children's illustration of a warm russet squirrel beside green woodland trees and one seed, no giant mascot. Use beautiful muted gouache-style illustration with ample clear space, arranged like a real picture book. On the lower middle of the page, large easy-to-read English serif text on two lines reading EXACTLY:
"The little squirrel lives in the forest."
"It finds a seed under a tree."
The word squirrel in the first sentence has a soft sage green rounded highlight. Closely adjacent to this highlighted word, a compact elevated definition card with a little pointer displays "squirrel", "松鼠", and a clear green circular play triangle followed by "听发音". It must visibly belong to the selected word and must not obscure other text.
Left narrow navigation: three realistic miniature page previews, current page bordered forest green and marked 3. Other visible page numbers 2 and 4. White space rather than heavy panel decoration.
Bottom: a compact floating audio playback bar in warm white with subtle shadow. It contains "听整页", previous-sentence icon, a prominent forest-green circular pause button indicating page playback, next-sentence icon, slim progress track, and "语速 0.8×". Controls have generous tap targets but no giant toolbar. The bar floats near the bottom edge without hiding page text.
Visual style: premium warm learning product, editorial and calm, precise real deployable UI proportions, friendly readable Chinese sans serif and a beautiful highly readable English book serif. Mostly paper white #F7F5ED, forest green #2C6250, sage #DCE8DC, a little apricot yellow in the illustration. Soft rounded corners, very restrained shadows, superb alignment, generous whitespace. Keep decor minimal, beautifully quiet. Chinese text should be accurate and sharply legible.
Constraints: Full single app screen, landscape approx 4:3, high fidelity, clean edges. No leaderboard, badges, points, rewards, course store, cloud sync, login, giant character, gradients, fake marketing headline, decorative title "森间书桌", watermarks, photography or device shell. The woodland illustration is inside the book content only; UI chrome stays calm.
